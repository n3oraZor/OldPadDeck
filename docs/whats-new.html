What's new

Every version, newest first.

From version 0.1.7 on, OldPadDeck tells you when a new version is out and installs it in one click: menu bar icon → Check for Updates…, and a dot on the iPad's ••• menu.

0.1.7

November 13, 2026Latest
  • OldPadDeck now updates itself. On its second launch, it asks whether to check for new versions automatically (once a day). When one is out, a blue dot appears on its Dock and menu bar icons, and Check for Updates… shows what changed and installs it in one click, then relaunches.
  • The iPad tells you too: a blue dot on the ••• button, and a line in its menu. Tap it to open the update window on the Mac.
  • Every update is signed, and the app refuses one that isn't. The check reads a small file on oldpaddeck.com and sends nothing about your Mac.
  • New on the website: What's new, with every version.

0.1.6

November 12, 2026
  • Send feedback from the app. The menu bar icon has a new Send Feedback… entry, and so do the Configure window and Settings → About on the iPad. It opens a short form at oldpaddeck.com/feedback: a thank-you, an idea or a problem, no account needed.
  • The app itself still sends nothing: it only opens that page in your browser, with the app and macOS versions in the address.

0.1.5

November 12, 2026
  • Drag widgets like on the iPad's Home Screen. Press and hold a widget, then move it: the widgets in the way make room. No need to open the edit screen first.
  • Fixes on the iPad: a key's label keeps exactly what you type; the Pomodoro and the track position no longer show a wrong time for a second; a city typed in Settings is applied when you close the sheet; Settings → About shows the right version.
  • Safer: a misbehaving AirPlay speaker on the network can no longer crash the app; a device you revoke stops being obeyed at once; only the deck's own page can use a pairing code.
  • Weather comes back within a minute when the Mac was offline at launch or wake.
  • Lighter on the Mac: with no iPad connected, OldPadDeck stops measuring and polling, so it costs next to nothing while the iPad sleeps.
  • Pairing can't be blocked by another device: wrong codes shut out the device that typed them (5 tries per code) instead of closing pairing for everyone.
  • Regional formats: 12-hour time and Fahrenheit where that's the habit. The Pomodoro stays right even when the iPad's clock is off.

0.1.4

November 9, 2026
  • Calendar and Reminders unlock right away. After you allowed access on the Mac, the Today widget could keep saying "access not granted" until OldPadDeck was restarted. Your events and reminders now show up within a minute, with no restart.

0.1.3

November 9, 2026
  • A clearer first run. Opening the app shows the pairing window until an iPad is connected. As soon as the iPad is paired, the window gives way to Configure Deck on its own.
  • Opening the app again (Dock icon, Finder, Spotlight) shows Configure Deck while an iPad is connected, and the pairing window otherwise.
  • Pair a Device now sits right under the iPad in Configure Deck, instead of at the bottom of the side bar.
  • The Configure Deck window can be moved again: drag it by its top edge, double-click to zoom.

0.1.2

November 9, 2026
  • After pairing, the next step is clear: the pairing window now offers Configure Deck, which opens the editor on the Mac, instead of only "Pair Another Device" (still there, just below).

0.1.1

November 9, 2026
  • The Capture key now opens the macOS Screenshot tool (the ⇧⌘5 toolbar), instead of a bare selection crosshair that was easy to miss from the iPad. Existing keys work as before, nothing to reconfigure.

0.1.0

November 9, 2026

First public version.

  • Six modes — Deck, Cockpit, Focus, Studio, Pad and Standby — that you can rearrange, resize and fill from a catalog of about two dozen widgets.
  • Controls: app and website keys, Shortcuts, key combos, a trackpad, volume and brightness sliders, window tiling.
  • Live Mac data: CPU per core, memory, GPU, temperatures and fans, power draw, network, battery and the batteries of your devices.
  • Focus: a Pomodoro timer, today's calendar and reminders you can check off.
  • Audio: now playing, and a picker for the Mac's output, Bluetooth and AirPlay speakers.
  • Automatic mode switching when you change apps or start a focus session.
