Things to try

Switch modesTap the tabs at the top: Deck, Cockpit, Focus, Studio, Pad, Standby.
Rearrange itTap ••• → Edit. Drag a widget, pull a corner to resize, or tap + Widget.
Pick a speakerIn Studio, open the audio output list and send the sound to an AirPlay speaker.
Plan your dayIn Focus: the Pomodoro, today's calendar and reminders you can check off.
Long-pressLong-press a process in Cockpit to quit it, or a widget to get its menu.
What's simulated: the Mac's numbers, apps, music, calendar and speakers are made up. In real life, the iPad talks to the OldPadDeck app on your Mac, and you pair it once with a code shown on the Mac.
